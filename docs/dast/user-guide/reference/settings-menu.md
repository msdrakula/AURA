# Settings menu

-

**Last updated: ** September 3, 2026
-

**Read time: ** 1 Minute

 The settings menu contains shortcuts to several of the configuration menus used in Burp Suite DAST. To access the settings menu, click the  symbol in the top-right corner of the window. Choose from the following options:


-  **Burp AI** - enable [Burp AI features](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/using-ai/burp-ai) for your instance of Burp Suite DAST.

-  **Scanning machines** - configure [scanning machines](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/managing-scanning-resources) and scanning pools.

-  **Scan configurations** - manage built-in and custom [scan configurations](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/managing-your-sites/site-settings/scan-configurations/custom-configs).

-  **Extensions** - add or edit [extensions](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/extensions) to implement custom scan behaviors and capabilities.

-  **Email** - [configure an SMTP server](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/post-installation-config/configure-smtp-server) to enable email notifications.

-  **Updates** - manage [updates](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide) for standard instances.

-  **Licensing** - review your current license, or upload a new license key.

-  **Network** - [configure your network settings](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/setup/self-hosted/standard/network-firewall-config).

-  **Integrations** - integrate Burp Suite DAST with [your CI/CD platform](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/ci-cd).

-  **Integrations** - integrate Burp Suite DAST with [your issue tracking platform](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/integrate-issue-tracking-platforms).

-  **Integrations** - integrate Burp Suite DAST with [Slack](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/integrating-slack).

-  **Site and scan data** - configure how [sites and the data generated by scans](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/working-with-scans/config-site-and-scan-data) are handled.

-  **User activity logs** - enable and download [user activity logs](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/reference/user-activity-logs).

-  **Database backup** - configure [database backups](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/dast/user-guide/post-installation-config/config-db-backup).

-  **Miscellaneous** - submit usage data about Burp Suite DAST.
