# Generating a report

-

**Last updated: ** September 3, 2026
-

**Read time: ** 1 Minute

 In this section, you'll learn how to generate a report in HTML format, based on your scan results.


### Step 1: Select the relevant issues

 Go to the **Target > Site map** tab, right-click on the entry for `https://ginandjuice.shop`, and select **Issues > Report issues for this host**.


![Select the relevant issues](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/desktop/images/getting-started/pro-qsg-7-report-for-host.png)

### Step 2: Configure the report options

 A wizard guides you through the available options, such as how much detail to include. For now, just click **Next** to accept the defaults until you're prompted to enter a filename and location for the report.


![Configure the report options](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/desktop/images/getting-started/pro-qsg-8-configure-report.png)

### Step 3: Generate and save the report

 Click **Select file** and choose a location where you want to save the report. Enter a name for the file.


#### Note

 You must include the appropriate file extension, in this case, `.html`.


 Click **Save** and then **Next** to generate the report.


### Step 4: View and share your report

 Open the report in Burp's browser to see what it contains. This is useful for reporting the results of your scans to colleagues or clients.


![Viewing the scan report](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/desktop/images/getting-started/quick-start-scanner-report.png)

 You've just generated a report of your findings.
