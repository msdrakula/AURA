# Burp Target tool

-

**Last updated: ** September 3, 2026
-

**Read time: ** 1 Minute

 The Target tool enables you to define which targets are in scope for your current work. In the **Site map**, you can toggle between the **URL view** and the **Crawl paths view**. These views show you detailed information about your target applications. You can use the information about your target application's content and functionality to drive the workflow for your [penetration testing](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/desktop/testing-workflow).
