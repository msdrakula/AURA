# AI features

-

**Last updated: ** September 3, 2026
-

**Read time: ** 1 Minute

 Burp Suite brings AI to your security testing in two complementary ways: Burp AT, which brings agentic AI to human-led pentesting, and Burp AI, which assists you within the Burp tools you already use.

## Burp AT

 Burp AT pursues the testing goals you set, planning and running the work itself, choosing the right actions for each step and keeping you in control throughout. Burp AT is available in Burp Suite Professional.

#### More information

 For more information, see [Burp AT](https://portswigger.net/burp/documentation/desktop/burp-at).


## Burp AI

 Burp AI brings AI assistance into individual Burp tools, to speed up and support your own manual testing. Burp AI is available in Burp Suite Professional and Burp Suite DAST, tailored to each product's workflows.

## Security and privacy

 We've designed our AI infrastructure with security, privacy, and transparency in mind:

-

**Full user control** - AI features only run when you choose, giving you full control over when and where they run.
-

**Data privacy** - AI request data is processed securely through our trusted AI infrastructure. It is never stored by our AI providers.
-

**Industry-standard security** - Our secure infrastructure complies with ISO 27001 standards and implements robust encryption, ensuring data is protected in transit and at rest.
